Notes:
- IMPORTANT: despite the column names, each "month" is NOAA's 2-MONTH average ending in that month (e.g. month 12 = November-December). Checked 2026-09-16 against NOAA's Indiana 1-month and 2-month time series: matches the 2-month series within 0.1 F in all 72 years for every month; also spot-checked for Alabama and Wyoming.
- monthly_mean_from_1901_to_2000 is therefore a 2-month normal, and alternates by 0.1 F between years for every state and month (likely rounding when the file was built).
- The time-scale setting is not recorded anywhere in the file or on the Kaggle page. This is the metadata lesson in Lecture 2.4.
