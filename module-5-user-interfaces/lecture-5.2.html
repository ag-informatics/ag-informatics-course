1. Journey map and use case

  • Map the full flow: how someone interacts with your Lab 4 web app, from start to finish.
  • Step by step: what they do, which page they're on, what data they see or enter.
  • (Think back to your task analysis in Lab 3)
  • Look for holes: step with no page = view to be built.

  • Then circle one task: as your use case. Fill in who and their goal at the top.
    Continue to Step 2 with this use case as your scope of work.

Spec kit: this is section 1 of your SPEC.md.

2. Pick 4 views to paper prototype

  • Based on your use case: pick 4 views.
    Look at the Lab 5 menu: 2 basic, 1 form, 1 advanced.

  • Build each view using the paper prototype kit's 12-column screen.
  • Front: two screens, one view each.
    Back: cut out the pieces you need (use your second copy for more).
  • Draw anything the kit doesn't have on the grid itself.


  • Done early? Swap with someone: they try the task, you play the "computer" and swap screens.
