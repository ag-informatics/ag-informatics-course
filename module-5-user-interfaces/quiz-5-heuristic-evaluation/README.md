# Quiz 5: Heuristic Evaluation

ASM 532 - Module 5 - Lecture 5.4

Decide what makes a tool "good" to you, compare that with Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics and with what farmers say they need, then use all three to review a real ag-tech tool.

This was an in-class quiz in Lecture 5.4, done in three steps across the lecture: the first two alone, the third in groups. It's how you test a design *without* users. Module 6 tests with them. The answers were discussed in class, in "What did you find?".

## The steps

1. **Your criteria (alone):** write down 5 criteria for what makes a tool "good" to you. The handout lists starters from the lecture's checklist.
2. **Yours vs. Nielsen's vs. farmers' (alone):** compare your criteria with Nielsen's heuristics and with the farmers' criteria from the lecture's technology assessments (the handout lists CAFF's 7 principles). Which of yours do they cover, which does neither cover, and what did they name that you missed? Circle what you'll add. Your criteria plus what you circled are your review list.
3. **Review farmOS (in groups):** pool your lists and agree on **5 criteria** as a group. Explore farmOS, and for each criterion rate it **works**, **partly**, or **breaks**, with what you saw. Then rank your top 3 problems and name one change you'd make.

Everyone hands in their own sheet.

The starter criteria are from Ankita Raturi's *How to build your digital toolbox* workbook (2026). The heuristics are Jakob Nielsen's [10 Usability Heuristics for User Interface Design](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/) (Nielsen Norman Group).
