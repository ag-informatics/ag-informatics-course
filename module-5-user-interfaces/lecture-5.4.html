Define your criteria

Tool qualities?

  • Utility: does the job "well"
  • Usability: easy to learn, user friendly
  • Affordability: up-front costs, subscription fees, training costs
  • Portability: can import/export data
  • Agency: no vendor-lock in
  • Repairability: whether DIY or low maintenance effort

How it's made?

  • Made "well"
  • Made by someone I trust? (farmers / food workers)
  • Knowing your toolbuilder matters
  • Can see "under the hood", whether open source or easy to open
  • Cooperatively produced / owned

Quiz 5, step 2: yours vs. Nielsen's vs. farmers'

Individually: Compare lists:


Yours Nielsen's Farmers'
your 5 criteria 10 usability heuristics CAFF's 7 principles

  • Which of yours do Nielsen or the farmers cover?
  • Which of yours does neither cover?
  • What did they name that you missed? Circle the ones you'll add.

Your criteria + what you circled = your review list.

Quiz 5, step 3: review farmOS

In groups. Everyone reviews the same app: a farmOS demo farm.

Log in: Juan's Alpaca Farm · username and password: manager

  1. Pool your lists, and agree on 5 criteria as a group.
  2. Explore farmOS. For each criterion: works, partly, or breaks, and what you saw.
  3. Rank your top 3 problems, worst first.
  4. Name one change you'd make, and which criterion it fixes.

Everyone hands in their own sheet. Handout: Quiz 5 · farmOS.org

What did you find?

  • What was your worst problem, and which criterion caught it?
  • Which 5 criteria did your group pick, and why those?
  • Would you pick farmOS? For which farmer?
